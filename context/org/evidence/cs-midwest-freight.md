# Midwest Freight Partners

## The pain

Midwest Freight had grown to ~900 loads/week on a brokerage team that was
drowning in status-chasing. Every carrier had its own portal; the ops team lived
in a shared inbox. The real constraint was **carrier onboarding** — six weeks of
manual EDI mapping and portal setup per carrier — which put a ceiling on growth.

## What we did

- Platform for 34 operational seats, partner portals for 120+ carriers.
- Implementation Services: discovery, two custom TMS connector builds, 13-month
  load-history backfill, exception-workflow setup for five exception types,
  training, and 30-day hypercare.
- Go-live 11 weeks after SOW signature.

## Results

- **Carrier onboarding cut ~40%**, from 6 weeks to 3.5, by turning EDI setup into
  a configured connector plus a self-service partner portal.
- **Loads per ops FTE up 22%** in the first quarter post-go-live, because the ops
  team stopped chasing status and started working the exception queue.
- Midwest Freight is a **named reference** and has taken three prospect calls.

## Why this deal is a good proof point

Services was the whole reason it worked. A software-only visibility tool would
have left Midwest Freight with the same six-week onboarding problem, because the
connectors and the history backfill were the hard part.
