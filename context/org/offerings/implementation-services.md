# Implementation Services

Every platform deal above $150k ACV includes an Implementation Services SOW. This
is the differentiator: the customer commits to a fixed scope and a
not-to-exceed price, and knows exactly what "live" means.

## Standard engagement shape

| Phase | Duration | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery | 2 weeks | Written integration design, signed off by the customer |
| Integration | 4–8 weeks | Connectors configured/built, data migrated, workflows set up |
| Managed onboarding | 2–4 weeks | Training delivered, go-live, 30-day hypercare |

Typical total: **10–14 weeks**. Priced on the rate card in `../pricing.md` with a
**fixed not-to-exceed**; overage requires a signed change order.

## Scope discipline

- **Two custom connectors are included.** A third is a change order.
- **Historical backfill is capped at 13 months.** More is a change order.
- **Hypercare is 30 days.** Ongoing managed services are a separate engagement,
  not part of this SOW, and are not something we currently sell well — do not
  commit to them in a deal without deal-desk review.
- **Acceptance is milestone-based** against the written integration design, with
  a defined review-and-cure cycle. We do not accept "sole discretion of the
  customer" acceptance language — see `../../legal/clause-library/termination.md`.
