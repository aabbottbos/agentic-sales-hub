# Rate card and discount authority

## Platform list pricing

- **Operational seat:** $145 / user / month, annual.
- **Read-only / partner seat:** $35 / user / month, annual.
- **Data volume:** $0.90 per 1,000 shipment-or-order events / month, with a
  committed monthly minimum negotiated per deal (typically $2,500–$8,000 for the
  mid-market).

## Services rate card

See frontmatter. Services are quoted as a fixed not-to-exceed built from a
staffing estimate against these rates. **The services floor is 15%** — no
services engagement is discounted more than that, regardless of platform
discount, because the engagement is already fixed-scope and thin-margin.

## Discount authority

| Approver | Max platform discount |
|---|---|
| Account Executive | 10% |
| VP Sales | 25% |
| CFO (recorded exception) | above 25% |

A discount above 25%, or any services discount above 15%, is a deal-desk
exception and must be recorded with a reason.
