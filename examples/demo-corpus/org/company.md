# Meridian Grid

Meridian Grid is a mid-market B2B company selling a supply-chain visibility
platform with a meaningful professional-services attach.

## What we sell

- **Meridian Grid Platform** — a SaaS platform for shipment and order visibility,
  exception management, and partner collaboration. Priced per seat plus a
  data-volume component. See `offerings/platform.md`.
- **Implementation Services** — a fixed-scope engagement (discovery, integration,
  managed onboarding) delivered against an SOW. Priced on a rate card with a
  fixed not-to-exceed. See `offerings/implementation-services.md`.

## How we win

We win deals where the customer has tried a pure-software visibility tool and
stalled at integration, or where they have the appetite for visibility but no
internal capacity to build it. Our wedge is that the services engagement is
scoped, priced, and committed up front — the customer knows exactly what "live"
means and when.

## How we lose

We lose to incumbents that are already embedded in the customer's TMS, to
"good enough" spreadsheet-and-email workflows when the pain isn't acute, and to
customers who want to buy software only and self-implement (we will sell platform
without services below $150k ACV, but it is not where we are strong).
